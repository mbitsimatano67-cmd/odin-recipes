ugali Recipes

description

Ugali Nyama is a popular Kenyan meal made by serving firm maize-meal ugali with a flavorful meat stew. The meat is usually cooked with onions, tomatoes, garlic, and spices until tender. The combination of soft, savory meat stew and firm ugali makes it a filling and satisfying meal.

Ingedient

  • 2 cups maize flour
  • 4 cups water
  • 500 g beef
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt
  • Seasoning
  • Coriander

steps to be followed

  1. Wash the beef and cut it into small pieces.
  2. Put the beef in a pot, add water and salt, and cook until the meat is tender.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a pan and fry the chopped onions until golden brown.
  4. Add minced garlic and cook for about one minute.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
  6. Add the cooked beef and seasoning, then mix well.
  7. Add some water or beef stock and simmer for 10–15 minutes.
  8. Add chopped coriander and stir.
  9. In a separate saucepan, bring 4 cups of water to a boil.
  10. Gradually add maize flour while stirring continuously.
  11. Keep stirring and adding flour until the ugali becomes thick and firm.
  12. Cover and cook the ugali on low heat for 5–10 minutes.
  13. Turn the ugali occasionally as it cooks.
  14. Serve the hot ugali with the nyama stew.
